# **App Name**: EmberFlow

## Core Features:

- Splash Screen: Display a splash screen with the app logo, a loading bar, and a gradient background with warm autumn-to-winter tones.
- Menu Screen: Show a menu screen with 12 character icons arranged in a 2x6 grid, with free characters fully colored and locked characters dimmed with a lock badge.
- Main Video Player: Play local video content in full HD within a large video area, including a digital clock overlay. Enable an auto-hiding preview bar at the bottom for quick video switching.
- IAP Integration: Incorporate Amazon IAP to allow users to unlock premium content, such as locked character icons, through in-app purchases.
- Notification Integration: Integrate OneSignal and Amazon ADM for sending seasonal updates and notifications directly to users on Fire TV or Tablet.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep orange (#C45901) for a warm and inviting feel, reminiscent of autumn evenings.
- Background color: Soft gray (#E0E0E0), desaturated, offering a calming backdrop that doesn't distract from the video content.
- Accent color: Navy blue (#0B3866) providing a professional contrast for highlights and interactive elements.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', serif, elegant and fashionable.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', sans-serif, modern, readable and functional.
- Use simple, minimalist icons with subtle glowing effects on focused elements to maintain a modern Guardian-style aesthetic.
- Employ smooth fades, slides, and hover effects for transitions and interactions to enhance the user experience without being distracting.
- Maintain a clean and organized layout with generous spacing and clear visual hierarchy, in line with Guardian-style minimalism.
